# Security and data incident response plan

## Severity
**Critical:** confirmed cross-customer disclosure, credential compromise with material access, destructive compromise or major loss of availability/data.
**High:** suspected unauthorised access or significant security weakness with realistic exploitation.
**Medium/Low:** contained issues with limited impact.

## Response
1. **Identify and preserve evidence.** Record detection time, reporter, affected services and known facts.
2. **Contain.** Revoke exposed credentials/tokens, restrict access, disable vulnerable functions or isolate services where appropriate.
3. **Assess.** Determine customers, records, personal data, time period and likely impact.
4. **Eradicate and recover.** Patch the cause, rotate credentials, restore/repair data and validate isolation.
5. **Notify.** Inform affected customers in line with contracts. Where Infinit Audit is a processor, provide the controller enough information to meet its UK GDPR duties. Escalate to legal/privacy advice when regulatory notification may be required.
6. **Review.** Complete a written post-incident review and track corrective actions.

## Incident log
Record incident ID, dates/times, reporter, severity, systems, affected tenants, data categories, containment, root cause, notifications, recovery, evidence and corrective actions.

Do not make unsupported assurances about impact before investigation is complete.
