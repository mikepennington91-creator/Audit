# Data Processing Agreement — template

**Status: commercial draft requiring legal review and provider details before signature.**

This DPA forms part of the agreement between the customer ("Controller") and the legal person/entity operating Infinit Audit ("Processor").

## 1. Processing
The Processor processes personal data only to provide, secure, support and maintain Infinit Audit and on documented instructions from the Controller, unless otherwise required by law.

## 2. Details
Subject matter: provision of quality-management and compliance software.
Duration: term of the service agreement plus agreed retention/deletion period.
Data subjects may include customer employees, contractors, suppliers, consumers/complainants and other persons whose details the Controller enters.
Data may include names, work contact details, roles, audit/action records, signatures, photographs, quality/traceability records and uploaded documents. Customers must not intentionally upload special-category or highly sensitive personal data unless this has been expressly agreed and appropriate safeguards are in place.

## 3. Confidentiality and security
The Processor will ensure authorised personnel are subject to confidentiality obligations and will maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures proportionate to risk, including access controls, encrypted transport, credential protection, tenant isolation, logging and backup controls.

## 4. Subprocessors
The Controller authorises the subprocessors identified in the current subprocessor register. The Processor remains responsible for imposing appropriate data-protection obligations on subprocessors. Material changes will be notified where required by the main agreement.

## 5. Assistance
Taking account of the nature of processing, the Processor will reasonably assist the Controller with data-subject requests, security obligations, breach assessment and data-protection impact assessments where applicable.

## 6. Personal data breaches
The Processor will notify the Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Controller data and provide available information reasonably required for the Controller's assessment and notifications.

## 7. International transfers
The Processor will use lawful safeguards for restricted transfers of personal data where required by UK data-protection law.

## 8. End of service
At the Controller's choice and subject to the agreement and applicable law, the Processor will return/export and/or delete personal data after the service ends. Backup copies may persist until normal backup expiry and remain protected during that period.

## 9. Audit information
The Processor will make reasonable information available to demonstrate compliance and cooperate with proportionate audits, subject to confidentiality, security and reasonable notice/cost arrangements.
