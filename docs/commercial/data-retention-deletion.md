# Data retention and deletion policy

Infinit Audit retains customer data only for the period required by the customer agreement, configured retention rules, legitimate operational requirements and applicable law.

## Customer records
The customer controls the business records it places in Infinit Audit. Contractual retention requirements should be agreed during onboarding. A default commercial retention period must be stated in the order form; do not silently delete quality records.

## Account data
Disabled/departed user accounts may be retained in a restricted form where their identity is needed to preserve audit trails. Authentication access must be removed promptly when requested by an authorised customer administrator.

## Customer termination
On termination:
1. Disable interactive access at the agreed date.
2. Offer an agreed export window.
3. Retain data for the contractual post-termination period where required.
4. Delete or irreversibly anonymise customer data after that period unless law or an agreed dispute hold requires continued retention.
5. Record completion of deletion.

## Backups
Deleted production data may remain in encrypted/provider backups until those backups naturally expire. It must not be restored for ordinary use after deletion; if a disaster restore reintroduces deleted data, the deletion process must be replayed.

## Evidence files
Evidence photographs and documents follow the retention period of the parent record unless a customer contract explicitly specifies otherwise.
